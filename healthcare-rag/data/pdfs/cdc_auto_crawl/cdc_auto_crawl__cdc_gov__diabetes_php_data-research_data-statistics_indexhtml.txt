At a glance

Access the U.S. Diabetes Surveillance System, the National Diabetes Statistics Report, and additional resources for key diabetes data and statistics.

U.S. Diabetes Surveillance System
The U.S. Diabetes Surveillance System is an interactive web tool that provides diabetes data at national, state, and county levels. It also provides data by age, sex, race/ethnicity, and education.

National Diabetes Statistics Report
See the full report with the latest data on diabetes incidence, prevalence, complications, cost, and more.
