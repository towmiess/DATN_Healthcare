National Diabetes Statistics Report

Jan. 21, 2026

At a glance

The National Diabetes Statistics Report provides up-to-date information on the prevalence and incidence of diabetes and prediabetes, risk factors for complications, acute and long-term complications, death, and costs.
Data in the report can help focus efforts to prevent and control diabetes across the United States. This report is continually updated as data become available. This report fulfills the requirement mandated by the Catalyst to Better Diabetes Care Act of 2009 (Section 10407 of Public Law 111-148).

Fast facts on diabetes

Undiagnosed diabetes

Prediabetes
115.2 million Estimated number of U.S. adults aged &ge;18 years with prediabetes
Type 1 diabetes
2.1 million Estimated number of people with diagnosed type 1 diabetes in the United States, including 1.8 million adults aged &ge;20 years and 314,000 children and adolescents younger than age 20 years
Prediabetes: &ge;65 years
31.3 million Estimated number of people aged 65 years or older (52.1%) with prediabetes
