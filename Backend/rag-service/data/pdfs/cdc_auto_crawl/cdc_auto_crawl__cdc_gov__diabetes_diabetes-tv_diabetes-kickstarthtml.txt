Diabetes Kickstart Video Series

At a glance

Did you know that people who receive diabetes education have a lower hemoglobin A1C? They also have a lower risk of diabetes-related health complications and depression.

Diabetes education saves you time, saves you money, and enhances your quality of life.

These seven animated videos will show you the self-care steps you need to get started managing diabetes. Watch them in any order—they all include tips and strategies you can use right now.
